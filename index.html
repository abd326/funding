01

Daily drawdown

Daily drawdown is the maximum permitted loss for a trading day. The proposed percentages must be calculated from a defined daily reference balance or equity, with the firm's approved reset time and treatment of floating P/L explicitly documented. A breach occurs when the applicable equity or balance measure reaches the stated threshold.

02

Maximum drawdown

Maximum drawdown is the total loss limit for the account. The firm must specify whether it is static or trailing, the reference amount, and whether open positions, commissions and swaps count. The proposed percentages alone do not establish those calculation mechanics.

03

Consistency requirements

No separate consistency score, best-day cap or lot-size restriction is configured in this draft. If the firm introduces one, it should publish the formula, measurement window, examples and consequences before a trader purchases a programme.

04

News & weekend holding

These settings are pending final policy approval. Until defined, traders should not assume that news trading, holding through market close or weekend exposure is permitted. Publish affected instruments, restricted windows and exceptions before launch.

05

EA & strategy rules

Automated strategies, copy trading, latency exploitation, account sharing and third-party execution need explicit written treatment. No blanket permission or prohibition is represented here; the final policy should define allowed tools and prohibited conduct.

06

Minimum days & time limits

Minimum trading days count days on which at least one qualifying trade is opened. The proposed maximum period is unlimited. The final firm rules should define server timezone, qualifying activity and treatment of partial trading days.

How do the challenges work?+

Choose an account size and an evaluation format. A standard challenge requires the trader to meet the relevant profit target while respecting the applicable risk limits. The proposed rules are shown on the Trading Rules page.

What is the difference between one phase and two phase?+

One phase uses a single proposed 10% profit target. Two phase uses an 8% target in Phase 1 and a 5% target in Phase 2. Both have proposed minimum trading days and drawdown limits as shown in the comparison table.

What is instant funding?+

Instant funding is a direct-access programme without a traditional evaluation profit target. It has its own proposed risk limits, including 3% daily drawdown, 6% maximum drawdown and leverage up to 1:50.

How is daily drawdown calculated?+

The final calculation method must be published by the firm, including the reference balance or equity, daily reset time, and treatment of floating profit and loss, fees and swaps. The percentage alone is not a complete formula.

How is maximum drawdown calculated?+

The proposed maximum drawdown figures are 8% for one phase, 10% for two phase and 6% for instant funding. Whether the limit is static or trailing and how equity is measured must be confirmed in the final policy.

Which payment methods are available?+

The checkout is configured for PayPal and USDT TRC20 only. A method is available for live payment only after the owner enters and verifies the relevant account details.

Why is USDT TRC20 discounted?+

The pricing configuration applies a 44.44% discount to USDT TRC20 payments, using $25 against the $45 reference price. PayPal remains at the full original price.

Is my payment verified when I submit a reference?+

No. Submitting a PayPal transaction ID or USDT transaction hash creates a payment submission for review. It does not verify the transfer, confirm receipt or guarantee programme activation.

When will my account be delivered?+

Account delivery should only take place after the firm has verified the payment and approved the order. This browser-only website cannot perform that verification automatically.

Are news trading, EAs and weekend holding allowed?+

These items are pending final policy approval in this proposed configuration. Check the final published rulebook before trading or purchasing.
